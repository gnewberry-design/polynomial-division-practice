🎯 Learning Target

I can divide a cubic polynomial by a linear binomial using an area model, and verify my quotient and remainder.

Why does it matter? Polynomial division helps us factor expressions, solve equations, and analyze functions.

Mastery: 80% or higher on your first attempts at each step.

📘 How to play: (1) Look at the highest-degree term. (2) Divide its coefficient by the leading coefficient of the divisor (1). (3) Type the number only, then click Check. (4) Repeat for the x², x, and constant terms. (5) Click the large Next Problem button after all three steps are correct.

Example: If the dividend starts with 3x³ and the divisor starts with x, the first quotient term is 3x², so enter 3.
